WHO WE ARE

A club for
those who paddle out.

You don't need perfect conditions to find your people.

NEBB is a home for bodyboarders across the North East, with our roots in Northumberland and our eyes on the North Sea.

Whether you're finding your feet in the whitewater or hunting down the next winter swell, the idea is simple: share the stoke, look out for each other, and enjoy the coast we call home.

02 / OUR COAST

A little wild.
A lot of heart.

Long open beaches, rugged reef breaks and a horizon that doesn't stop. From the Northumberland coastline to the wider North East, this stretch of sea is what brings us together.

We respect changing conditions, local knowledge and the places we ride. The North Sea gives plenty, but never on demand.
